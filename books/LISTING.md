# KDP listing: Great British Large Print Word Search

Files to upload (in `books/out/`):
- **Manuscript:** `wordsearch-interior.pdf` (68 pages, 8.5 × 11 in, no bleed, fonts embedded)
- **Cover:** `wordsearch-cover.pdf` (full wrap 17.403 × 11.25 in, including 0.125 in bleed; spine calculated for 68 pages on white paper)

Rebuild them any time with `python3 books/make_wordsearch.py`.

## Step by step (about 15 minutes)

1. Sign in at <https://kdp.amazon.com> with an Amazon account, then complete **tax and bank details**. This is the one step only you can do.
2. **+ Create → Paperback.**
3. **Paperback details:** copy the fields below.
4. **Paperback content:**
   - ISBN: *Get a free KDP ISBN*
   - Print options: **Black & white interior, white paper**, trim **8.5 × 11 in**, bleed **No bleed**, cover finish **Matte**
   - Upload the manuscript, then the cover (choose *Upload a cover you already have*)
   - AI-generated content: answer **Yes** for text. The word lists were written with AI assistance and the grids are computer-generated. Amazon requires this disclosure; it isn't shown to buyers.
   - Open the **Previewer** and fix anything it flags. It should pass as-is.
5. **Pricing:** see below, then **Publish**. Review usually takes up to 72 hours.
