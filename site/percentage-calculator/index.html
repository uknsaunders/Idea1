Percentage formulas

  • X% of Y = X ÷ 100 × Y
  • X as a percent of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • Percentage change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Common uses: discounts and sale prices, tips, VAT and sales tax, pay rises, investment returns and exam scores.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Multiply by (1 + percent/100). For example, 200 plus 15% is 200 × 1.15 = 230.

Why isn't a 50% drop undone by a 50% rise?

Because the rise is calculated on the smaller number. 100 falls 50% to 50, and 50 rising 50% is only 75.
